The Paradigm Shift

What is CardioGlyc AI?

CardioGlyc AI is a dynamic, closed-loop CardioMetabolic Digital Twin that harmonizes longitudinal Electronic Health Records (EHR) with continuous real-time streaming wearables to construct a biophysically grounded virtual replica of the patient's pancreas, heart, and vasculature.

1. Multi-Modal Data Fusion

Ingests static EHR records (ICD-10, HbA1c, lipids) combined with dense time-series wearable streams (CGM, PPG, ABPM, METs).

2. Continuous Biophysical ODEs

Solves Runge-Kutta numerical equations for the Bergman Minimal Model and Two-Element Windkessel hemodynamics.

3. 42-Minute Proactive Early Warning

Forecasts severe postprandial spikes (>220 mg/dL), hypoglycemia (<70 mg/dL), and hypertensive crises with ROC-AUC 0.914.

4. "What-If" Counterfactual Sandbox

Enables doctors and patients to simulate the exact physiological impact of walking, dietary changes, and medication adherence.

Scientific Rigor

Physiological Differential Equations

Bergman Minimal Model (Endocrine)
dG/dt = -(p1 + X + U_ex)G + p1*Gb + Ra(t)
dX/dt = -p2*X + p3*(I - Ib)

Models gut carb absorption decay (kAbs) and non-insulin mediated muscle GLUT-4 glucose clearance during physical activity (U_ex).

Two-Element Windkessel (Hemodynamics)
MAP = CO * R = (SV * HR) * R
PP = SBP - DBP ≈ SV / C

Simulates systemic vascular resistance (R) and arterial compliance (C) to capture arterial stiffness and sodium-induced surges.

*Why this matters:* Anchoring deep learning within differential equations eliminates hallucination and guarantees biophysically plausible trajectory forecasts.
